# TrackHit App Store mockups (review)

Imagine-generated **marketing slides** sized for the iPhone **6.5"** slot currently shown in App Store Connect.

Your Connect drop zone accepts any of:

| Orientation | Pixels |
|---|---|
| Portrait | **1242 × 2688** or **1284 × 2778** |
| Landscape | **2688 × 1242** or **2778 × 1284** |

Use **one orientation for the whole set**. Mix portrait and landscape in the same 6.5" shelf and Connect will reject the mismatched files.

## Folders

- `portrait-1242x2688/` — 5 slides, the size listed first in your screenshot
- `portrait-1284x2778/` — same 5, iPhone 12–14 Pro Max 6.5"
- `landscape-2688x1242/` and `landscape-2778x1284/` — 2 wide slides
- `source/` — original 9:16 / 16:9 Imagine frames before padding

## Do not upload these as the only App Store screenshots

Apple 2.3.3 wants the **real TrackHit UI**. Imagine invents the in-phone screens (garbled labels like TRACKMT, Bencin Press, Puch Day). These are for **layout / color / copy review**.

For Connect:

1. Capture real screens on device (Mock data off)
2. Optionally composite those captures into these frames
3. Upload 3–10 portrait JPEGs from `portrait-1242x2688/` once the phone UI is real

App Previews (the video slots) are separate — these JPEGs are screenshots only.
