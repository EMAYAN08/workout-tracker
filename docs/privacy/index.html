Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Emayan Vadivel (“TrackHit”, “we”, “I”) develops TrackHit, a workout logger designed to stay out of the way. This policy describes what the iOS app does with information. It is written in plain language for App Store review and for you.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the TrackHit iOS app and to this website (emayan08.github.io/workout-tracker). The website is a static GitHub Pages site. It does not set advertising cookies and it does not run an account system.

Data we collect

None from the app. TrackHit does not create accounts, does not ask for your name or email, and does not send workout data to a server. We do not operate a cloud backend for this product.

The following stay on your iPhone, in on-device storage, until you delete them:

  • Workout history (exercises, sets, weight, reps, duration, timestamps)
  • Routines and custom exercises
  • Settings: units (lb/kg), rest duration, appearance, accent colors
  • An optional in-progress session so a workout can resume after you leave the app

These values never leave the phone unless you export a backup file or share an image through the iOS share sheet.

Notifications

Rest-timer alerts are optional and scheduled locally with Apple’s notification APIs. TrackHit does not use Apple Push Notification service to message you from a server. We do not send marketing.

How to delete

In the app: Settings → Delete all data. That wipes workouts, routines, and custom exercises on this device. Theme and units may remain. Deleting the app also removes local data.

Export and import

You can export a JSON backup and import it on another phone you own. TrackHit does not upload that file. iOS presents the system share sheet; the destination (Files, Mail, AirDrop) is your choice.

Share from History or a finished workout creates an image or PDF on device, then hands it to the share sheet. We do not host those files.

Third parties

The app does not include analytics SDKs, ads, crash reporters, social logins, or tracking pixels. Distribution may involve Apple (App Store, TestFlight). This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard server logs as described in GitHub’s own privacy statement. We do not sell information. We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).

Website feedback

If you post feedback on this site, the note is created as a public GitHub issue on the TrackHit repository so we can acknowledge it. Do not include personal health details, passwords, or anything you do not want public. You may email instead: emayanramalingam@gmail.com.

Children

TrackHit is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no account creation.

Health information

TrackHit is a personal training log. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent disease. It does not connect to HealthKit or a wearable in the current version.

Security

Because data stays on the device, it is protected by your iPhone passcode and iOS app sandbox. We take reasonable care with this website. No method of storage is perfectly secure.

Your rights

You already hold the data. Access it in the app. Export it. Wipe it. There is no profile on our side to request because we do not have one.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after an update means you have read the new version.
